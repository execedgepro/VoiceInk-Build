## 📦 PRODUCTS

### Product 1 — Mastering Elite Focus At Work (eBook)
**Format:** Digital eBook — workshop in book form
**What it is:** The complete Elite Focus Operating System —
five chapters, four rules, one complete system for elite
professional performance.
**Three tiers:**
| Tier | Price | What's included |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Core eBook | $29 | Complete programme — 5 chapters, 4 rules, EFOS |
| Implementation Kit | $49 | Everything + 6 tools: Self-Audit, Attention Residue Exercise, Focus Ritual Builder, Daily Scheduler, Low-Value Work Planner, 30-Day Challenge Tracker |
| Coach & Trainer Edition | $159 | Everything + facilitation package: Facilitation Guide, Workshop Assessments, Slide Deck, Discussion Prompts, Marketing Templates, Email Sequences, License to deliver workshops |

**Distribution:** Gumroad (recommended)
**Key science hooks:**
- 74 times/day — average professional checks email
- 23 minutes — time to regain full focus after interruption
- 2.5 hours — meaningful work produced in average 8-hour day
- Attention Residue — the hidden focus killer

### Product 2 — EliteWrite (macOS App)
**What it is:** AI-powered voice-to-text transcription app
for macOS. Runs entirely on-device — no internet needed.
**Key feature:** Focus Session Mode — 25-minute timed focus
blocks with Focus Score (0-100) and coaching notes
**Model:** Free download (GPL v3.0 open source)
**Positioning:** The tool that brings the eBook to life
**URL:** execedgepro.com/elitewrite (sub-brand page)
**Download:** Direct from execedgepro.com (not App Store)
**Tagline bridge:** "Read the system. Use the tool.
Live the transformation."
**Key selling point on Apple Silicon:** Near-instant
transcription with Parakeet V2 model
