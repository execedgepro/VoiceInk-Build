## 🏗️ WEBSITE STRATEGY & ARCHITECTURE

### Chosen Direction (confirmed by founder — 2026-05-21)
**Scenario 1+3 with future-proof architecture**

The website is a BRAND HOME — not just a sales page for
one product. It must:
1. Convert visitors to buyers or email subscribers TODAY
2. Accommodate new products, new audiences, and new revenue
   streams TOMORROW — without rebuilding anything

### Future-Proof Architecture Rules (Eng. Kael)
- Navigation built to expand:
  Products (dropdown) · About · Insights · [future items]
- A single shared CSS shell: header + footer + colour system
  inherited by every page automatically
- No hardcoded colours or fonts — defined once in CSS
  variables, applied everywhere
- EliteWrite treated as a growing sub-brand, not an afterthought
- Products section designed as a hub — new products plug in

| Content Moat | Insights/Articles — Google + email fuel | Phase 2 |
